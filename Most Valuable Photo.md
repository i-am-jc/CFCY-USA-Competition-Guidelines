**MOST VALUABLE PHOTO (MVP) COMPETITION**

*Let?s face it, with the accessibility of posting pictures online using social networks, we cannot help but  contribute to capturing and viewing our peers on our own photographs. Why not take a picture of an  image that reminds us of how amazing and vast God?s love is for us? Each captured image tells a story.  Are you ready to share yours?* 

**RULES & REGULATIONS** 

1. Capture a moment or image that is Christ-centered and depicts anything related to the CFC 2026 theme "As One" anchor verse of **1 John 1:3-4**, which says, 

**3** What we have seen and heard we announce to you also, so that you will join with us in the fellowship that we have with the Father and with his Son Jesus Christ. **4** We write this in order that our joy may be complete.

2\. Must be an active CFC Youth member within the age bracket of 12 to 21 years old (as of the date of YCON). 

3\. Must be registered for the YCON  

4\. No computer-generated enhancements will be allowed (i.e., photoshop). 

5\. Only camera functions allowed (i.e., sepia, black and white, other camera filters) 

6\. Must provide a 1-paragraph write-up that is no more than 5 sentences long. The write-up must  answer the following questions: 

a. ***What has inspired you to capture the image?*** 

b. ***How does this picture relate to the ANCHOR VERSE?***

**ENTRY & SUBMISSION DETAILS** 

? Only one entry per region is permitted

? Each entry must be submitted as a .png or .jpeg file with a 1-paragraph write-up via email to [cfcycomps@gmail.com](mailto:cfcycomps@gmail.com) for the Creatives Committee. Please use the subject headline: ?Most Valuable Photo 2026:  (AREA/CLUSTER/CHAPTER), (NAME)? 

? On the day of YCON: Submit a printed copy of the 1-paragraph write-up
