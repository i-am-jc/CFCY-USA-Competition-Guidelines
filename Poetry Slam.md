**POETRY SLAM COMPETITION** 

1\. All participants should submit their final entry form at least 2 weeks before the start of YCON. The Creatives Committee should be notified ahead of time for any changes that will be  made prior to the YCON date. **Any revisions made on the day of will not be accepted.** 

2\. They should be verified by **the CFC Youth Area/Cluster/Chapter Couple Coordinator and/or  Area/Cluster/Chapter Heads** to ensure that they are **active CFC Youth members**. An entry form  missing an/a Area/Cluster/Chapter Couple Coordinator?s and/or Area/Cluster/Chapter Head?s  approval will not be accepted. This form must be filled out and sent to the Creatives Committee  via email. 

3\. All participants must be registered for YCON. 

4. Your poem must represent the CFC 2026 theme "As One" anchor verse of **1 John 1:3-4**, which says, 

**3** What we have seen and heard we announce to you also, so that you will join with us in the fellowship that we have with the Father and with his Son Jesus Christ. **4** We write this in order that our joy may be complete.

5\. Your written poem must be of a positive and/or Catholic nature. Content that is sexually  suggestive or vulgar will result in **disqualification**. 

6\. The poem can be no longer than 4 minutes in length. 

7\. All poems must be performed a cappella. 

8\. No additional members are allowed during the performance.

9\. Please send the poem lyrics in presentation format via email to the Creatives Committee at least 2 weeks before the start of YCON. Also, please **provide a copy of the lyrics** for the Creatives Committee on the day of the YCON with the poem title and writer.  

10\. The lyrics of the poem must be handed to the Program Committee at least one hour before the  competition for them to have a chance to read the lyrics beforehand. 

11\. The poem that is performed must be the same as the lyrics handed to the Creatives Committee. 

12\. It is the performer?s responsibility to signify his or her presence at least **one hour** before the  competition. He or she needs to present a copy of his or her poem lyrics to the Creatives  Committee at least one hour before his or her performance. Participants must pay attention for  any announcements during the YCON. Their performance order will be announced before the  competition. 

13\. Each Poetry Slam participant must submit a minimum **1-paragraph write-up** of the inspiration  behind the poem, which must be submitted via email [the Creatives Committee](mailto:the Creatives Committee)  to the Creatives Committee at least 2  weeks before the start of the YCON. 

**Criteria for Judging**

**Theme Relevance and Content 40%**

Connection to 1 John 1:3-4 "As One" 20%

Message and Content Quality 20%

**Poetic Craft 30%**

Literary Technique and Creativity 15%

Word Choice and Imagery 15%

**Performance and Delivery 30%**

Voice, Tone, and Expression 15%

Stage Presence and Confidence 15%

***TOTAL 100%***

**JUDGING GUIDANCE**

For a 10-point item, start at 8 points as the baseline for a good entry. Use the following scale:
- 10 points: Exceptional, exceeded expectations
- 9 points: Very good, above average
- 8 points: Good, meets expectations (baseline)
- 7 points: Fair, slightly below expectations
- 6 or below: Needs improvement

**DEDUCTIONS**

• Late entry form submission, including 1-paragraph write-up (5 points)
• Over the time limit (2 points per 30 seconds over)
• Failure to provide lyrics in presentation format (5 points)
• Performance does not match submitted lyrics (5 points)
• Inappropriate or vulgar content (disqualification)
