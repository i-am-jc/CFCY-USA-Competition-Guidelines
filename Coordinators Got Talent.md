**Couple Coordinators Got Talent** 

1\. The competition is open to all CFC members and non-CFC Parent Support Group (PSG) volunteers. 

2\. All participants should submit their entry form with a description of their talent at least 2 weeks  before the date of YCON. The Creatives Committee should be notified ahead of time for any  changes that will be made prior to the YCON date. Any revisions made on the day of will not be  accepted. 

3\. Each group must be composed of a minimum of 1 solo Couple Coordinator and a maximum of 16  people (ideally 8 couples). 

4\. The Area/Cluster/Chapter Couple Coordinators (CCs) should verify the official roster. Entry forms  missing the Area/Cluster/Chapter Couple Coordinator?s approval will not be accepted. 

5\. Entries with group submissions must be composed of MIXED performers, that is, brothers and sisters  (there should be more than 1 brother/sister in a group). 

6. All CCs Got Talent captains must submit a minimum **1-paragraph write-up** about how their performance portrays the CFC 2026 theme "As One" anchor verse of **1 John 1:3-4**, which says, 

**3** What we have seen and heard we announce to you also, so that you will join with us in the fellowship that we have with the Father and with his Son Jesus Christ. **4** We write this in order that our joy may be complete.

This should be submitted along with the mp3 recording and any other materials being used  at least 2 weeks before the date of the YCON. 

7\. The performance must be a minimum of 2 minutes and a maximum of 5 minutes in length. 

8\. The routine must incorporate the **anchor verse**. 

9\. Performance should be wholesome and lively. MOVEMENTS MUST NOT BE VULGAR OR SEXUALLY  SUGGESTIVE.

10\. Costumes and props must be DECENT AND PROPER for the performance. The Creatives Committee  reserves the right to not allow the performance of a group due to costume or prop technicalities.  

11\. It is the group?s responsibility to signify its presence at least **one hour** before the competition. 

12\. A mandatory meeting will be set up for all CCs Got Talent Captains to be briefed on guidelines, the  process on the day of the competition, and their order in performance.
