**DANCE SHOWCASE**

1\. All participants should submit their final entry form at least 2 weeks before the start of YCON. The Creatives Committee should be notified ahead of time for any changes that will be  made prior to the YCON date. **Any revisions made on the day of will not be accepted.** 

2\. The **CFC-Youth Area/Cluster/Chapter Couple Coordinator and/or Area/Cluster/ Chapter Heads** must verify the official list to ensure they are **active CFC Youth members**. An entry form missing  an/a Area/Cluster/Chapter Couple Coordinator?s and/or Area/Cluster/Chapter Head?s approval  will not be accepted. This must be sent to the Creatives Committee via email.

3\. All participants must be registered for YCON. 

4\. Dance captains are required to write a minimum **1-paragraph write-up** about why they chose  the pre-chosen track and how the choreography and overall performance portrays the theme,  which must be submitted by its respective deadline. **Late or no submission may not be accepted.** 

5\. Each dance group must be composed **of 5-15 members**. **Members should be within the age bracket of 12 to 21 years old (as of the date of YCON).** Entries must also be composed of **MIXED** dancers, that is, brothers and sisters (there should be more than one brother/sister in a  group). 

6\. The performance must be a minimum of 2 minutes and a maximum of 5 minutes in length (from the start to the end of music played). 

7. The team must somehow interpret the CFC 2025 theme anchor verse of **Bible verse Matthew 28:8 & 10 NABRE**, which says, 

*?**8** Then they went away quickly from the tomb, fearful yet overjoyed, and ran to announce[[a]](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%2028%3A8-10&version=NABRE#fen-NABRE-28523a) this to his disciples? **10** Then Jesus said to them, ?Do not be afraid. Go tell my brothers to go to Galilee, and there they will see me.?*

8\. The routine must incorporate TWO UNIFYING SPECIFICATIONS: 

1. Each team must work with at least ONE track from a Worship artist. Examples include Liveloud, Matt Maher, and Audrey Assad.  
2. The pre-chosen track must be at least 45  seconds of the duration of the mix. Anything less will be penalized. 

9\. THERE ABSOLUTELY MUST NOT BE ANY MOVEMENTS THAT ARE VULGAR OR SEXUALLY  SUGGESTIVE. 

10\. Costumes must be DECENT AND PROPER for the dance. The Creatives Committee reserves the right to not allow the performance of a group due to costume technicalities. 

11\. Music should be wholesome and lively. **Subliminal, sexually suggestive, and/or vulgar  lyrics/language are not be allowed**. Non-compliance with this guideline will result in  **disqualification**. 

12\. Your final mix in mp3 format MUST be submitted via email to [cfcycomps@gmail.com](mailto:cfcycomps@gmail.com) at least 2 weeks before the start of YCON. Your music mix  must only be one track and not divided into several tracks. The mix should be named after your Area/Cluster/Chapter and creative showcase and sent to the Creatives Committee. 

13\. On the day of the showcase, you must bring your **USB** as a backup. It must be the same as the  mp3 mix and be labeled with your delegation and showcase. 

14\. It is the team?s responsibility to signify its presence at least **one hour** before the showcase.  Dance teams who will not comply will risk disqualification. Participants must pay attention to any announcements during YCON. Their performance order will be announced before the  showcase. 

15\. A mandatory meeting will be set up for all Dance Captains to be briefed on guidelines, the  process on the day of the showcase, and their order in performance.
