GENERAL INFORMATION

Here are national guidelines in order to ensure the unity of its implementation regarding competitions for CFC Youth USA:

Active CFC Youth Member Definition

For the purpose of National Youth Conference, an active CFC Youth member means that a CFC Youth member should have attended at least one official CFC Youth activity in the past half-year (Month of your YCON minus 6 months), including any CFC activity that involves the participation of CFC Youth members (e.g., CFC General Assembly, CFC Evangelization Rally), but excluding fellowships. The only exceptions are for new CFC Youth members who join after the past half year and non-members as a means of "gateway evangelization" (the latter upon the discretion of the Area/Cluster/Chapter).

Non-CFCY Member Participation

This YCON will be used as an Evangelization Gateway opportunity. Hence, non-CFCY can participate in the competitions with the following conditions:

  1. The non-member is >= 12 years old and <= 16 years old.
  2. For any team competition, the team MUST have a combination of CFCY and NON-CFCY members. There should be more CFCY-members than NON-CFCY members.
  3. Should the non-CFCY participant win, they can attend the National Conference and compete (if they are part of a sports team) or present their winning entry (for Creative Competitions)

National Competitions: WINNERS OF THE PreCon

At the CFC Youth USA National Conference (YCON), there will be both Sports and Creative Competitions. The participants will be the respective winners of each PreCon (Regional Youth Conference). The winners of each competition at PreCon will represent their region at YCON, which will be considered as the "championships".

At least 50% of each winning team of each PreCon must compete for sports at YCON. If the team/individual who won at YCON cannot attend, then the opportunity to compete should be given to the 2nd place winner or other members from the region willing to participate.

BANNER COMPETITION

  1. All participants should submit their final entry form at least 2 weeks before the start of YCON. The Creatives Committee should be notified ahead of time for any changes that will be made prior to the YCON date. Any revisions made on the day of will not be accepted.
  2. The CFC Youth Area/Cluster/Chapter Couple Coordinators and/or Area/Cluster/Chapter Heads must verify the official list to ensure that they are active CFC Youth members. An entry form missing an/a Area/Cluster/Chapter Couple Coordinator's and/or Area/Cluster/Chapter Head's approval will not be accepted. This must be sent to the Creatives Committee via email.
  3. All participants must be registered for YCON.
  4. You are required to provide a minimum 1-paragraph write-up explaining the concept of your decisions as to how your design reflects the theme. This should be submitted to the Creatives Committee at least 2 weeks before the start of YCON. Late or no submission may not be accepted for the competition.
  5. Your banner must represent the CFC 2026 theme "As One" anchor verse of 1 John 1:3-4.
  6. The banner must be vertical (portrait orientation) 2 meters high (approximately 2 yards) and 1 meter wide (approximately 1 yard).
  7. Teams will be required to provide and construct a support system made out of PVC pipes and Velcro, wood, paint roller extension poles, or other means to hold it up straight, tall, and sturdy). It is your banner representative's responsibility to set this up before the Banner Parade and to take it down at the end of the day.
  8. It must also have the name of your region clearly on the banner.
  9. Teams can be composed of up to 10 members.

Criteria for Judging

Creativity and Design 30% Originality and Artistic Merit (15%), Visual Impact and Color Use (15%)
Technical Execution 30% Craftsmanship and Quality (15%), Overall Presentation (15%)

Deductions

  • Late entry form submission, including 1-paragraph write-up: 5 points
  • Missing region name on banner: 3 points

Your music mix must only be one track and not divided into several tracks. The mix should be named after your Area/Cluster/Chapter and creative competition and sent to the Creatives Committee.
  13. On the day of the competition, you must bring your USB as a backup. It must be the same as the mp3 mix and be labeled with your delegation and competition.
  14. It is the team's responsibility to signify its presence at least one hour before the competition. Dance teams who will not comply will risk disqualification. Participants must pay attention to any announcements during YCON. Their performance order will be announced before the competition.
  15. A mandatory meeting will be set up for all Dance Captains to be briefed on guidelines, the process on the day of the competition, and their order in performance.

BAND COMPETITION

  2. The CFC-Youth Area/Cluster/Chapter Couple Coordinator and/or Area/Cluster/Chapter Heads must verify the official list to ensure they are active CFC Youth members. An entry form missing an/a Area/Cluster/Chapter Couple Coordinator's and/or Area/Cluster/Chapter Head's approval will not be accepted. This must be sent to the Creatives Committee via email.
  3. All participants must be registered for YCON.
  4. The band must consist of instrumentalists and vocalists. There is no minimum or maximum to the amount of instrumentalists and vocalists, but there is a limit to the amount of inputs and microphones. Amps will not be switched out, so if the team has an instrument requiring a different plug-in, a vocal microphone must be used instead.
  5. All band members must be between 12 to 21 years old as of July 31, 2026.
  6. The Creatives Committee will provide as part of the sound system all amplifiers and at least 1 bass, 2 acoustic guitars, 1 electric guitar, 1 keyboard, 6 vocal microphones, and 1 set of drums per participating band. The participating bands must provide for additional instruments and other gadgets if necessary (e.g., violin, viola, trumpet, flute, saxophone, ukulele).
  7. NOTE: If your band instrumentalist prefers to use his or her own instrument or has specific instrument/equipment use, please notify the Creatives Committee far in advance before the competition. Instruments that require a different amp than the ones provided must use a vocal microphone instead.
  8. Each band is required to play a medley of 3 songs. The order in which these will appear is up to the discernment of the band:
    • Remixed Worship Song (not performed in its original composition)
  9. Secular Songs:
    1. One of the secular songs must be about the CFC 2026 theme "As One" anchor verse of 1 John 1:3-4.
    2. One of the secular songs must be reinvented in its format. (i.e., change of beat, fast to slow song, added instrumentals, etc.)
    3. IMPORTANT: Lyrics absolutely cannot have vulgarity or insinuations of anything ungodly.
  10. Presentation should be a minimum of 5 to a maximum of 6 minutes.
  11. Each band will also be given 2 minutes to set up before they perform. This could take place during another performance.
  12. The band look, which includes the attire of the members, must be decent and proper.
  13. Music lyrics should be provided for all songs selected. A copy of each song will be needed for the Creatives Committee on the day of the competition. Only the lyrics being used are required.
  14. It is the band's responsibility to signify its presence at least one hour before the competition. The band needs to present its music lyrics to the Creatives Committee at least one hour before its performance. Participants must pay attention for any announcements during YCON. Their performance order will be announced before the competition.
  15. All team captains must submit a minimum 1-paragraph write-up explaining how the song choices exemplify the anchor verse. This will be sent via email to the Creatives Committee at least 2 weeks before the start of YCON.

MOST VALUABLE PHOTO (MVP) COMPETITION

Let's face it, with the accessibility of posting pictures online using social networks, we cannot help but contribute to capturing and viewing our peers on our own photographs. Why not take a picture of an image that reminds us of how amazing and vast God's love is for us? Each captured image tells a story. Are you ready to share yours?

Rules & Regulations

  1. Capture a moment or image that is Christ-centered and depicts anything related to the CFC 2026 theme "As One" anchor verse of 1 John 1:3-4.
  2. Must be an active CFC Youth member between 12 to 21 years old as of July 31, 2026.
  3. Must be registered for the YCON
  4. No computer-generated enhancements will be allowed (i.e., photoshop).
  5. Only camera functions allowed (i.e., sepia, black and white, other camera filters)
  6. Must provide a 1-paragraph write-up that is no more than 5 sentences long. The write-up must answer the following questions:
    • What has inspired you to capture the image?
    • How does this picture relate to the ANCHOR VERSE?

Criteria for Judging

Category Percentage Details
Theme Relevance and Message 45% Connection to 1 John 1:3-4 "As One" (25%), Message Clarity and Impact (20%)
Photographic Quality 30% Composition and Framing (15%), Lighting and Technical Quality (15%)
Creativity and Originality 25% Artistic Vision (15%), Originality (10%)

Deductions

  • Late submission: 5 points
  • Computer-generated enhancements detected: disqualification
  • Missing or incomplete write-up: 5 points
  • Write-up exceeds 5 sentences: 2 points

ORIGINAL VIDEO COMPETITION

Film has a unique and powerful way of capturing life and conveying a message. This year we are challenging you to use your God-given talents to express the New Evangelization! "Evangelization will always contain as the foundation, center and, at the same time, the summit of its dynamism-- a clear proclamation that in Jesus Christ, salvation is offered to all men, as a gift of God's grace and mercy. The new evangelization is not a matter of merely passing on doctrine but rather of a personal and profound meeting with the Savior." – Blessed John Paul II

Create an original video that can be used as a way to evangelize – by showing them on social media platforms and at varying CFCY events.

Rules & Regulations

  1. The original video must somehow interpret the CFC 2026 theme "As One" anchor verse of 1 John 1:3-4.
  2. Accepted original video submissions types:
    • Narrative - Tells a fictional or fictionalized story, event or narrative. E.g. Spirit Rangers
    • Awareness video - The goals of an awareness video include: Draw attention to key issues that matter to you, raise awareness about who you are and what you do, inspire people to act, educate people, share a story of change, share first hand accounts.
    • Commercial - A video that usually promotes a product, service or brand. E.g. Cingular Parody, Becoming a Catholic Nun
  3. The Original Video can be as short as 1 minute, but it must be under 3 minutes and 15 seconds.
  4. No one over 21 years of age is allowed to help with any filming, editing, or production.
  5. There is no age limit for the persons in the film (e.g., grandparents, parents, etc.).
  6. REMINDER: Before filming inside a Church, ask permission from the parish or priest. Always be respectful and reverent inside a Church – Jesus is truly present in the Blessed Sacrament.

Criteria for Judging

Theme Relevance and Message 40% Connection to 1 John 1:3-4 "As One" (20%), Message Clarity and Evangelization Impact (20%)
Creativity and Storytelling 35% Originality and Concept (15%), Storytelling and Flow (20%)
Technical Quality and Production 25% Video and Audio Quality (10%), Editing and Production Value (15%)

Deductions

  • Late submission: 5 points
  • Over the time limit: 2 points per 15 seconds over
  • Under 1 minute: 3 points
  • Evidence of adult help in filming/editing/production: disqualification
  • Inappropriate content: disqualification
  • Missing or incomplete write-up: 5 points

BASKETBALL (BOYS AND GIRLS)

  1. Consists of active CFC Youth members with a maximum of 15 players per team. The teams are divided into brothers and sisters. All players must be between 12 to 18 years old as of July 31, 2026. All players must play in the game. If all players do not play, the other team will receive 15 points at the end of the game.
  2. All rosters are due at least 2 weeks before the start of YCON.
  3. All Rosters must be reviewed and approved by the CFC-Youth Area/Cluster/Chapter Couple Coordinators to ensure that the team consists entirely of active CFC Youth members (as defined in the General Information section). Entry forms missing the Area/Cluster/Chapter Couple Coordinator's approval will not be accepted.
  4. All participants must be registered for YCON.
  5. Please assign one Couple Coordinator and one CFC Youth leader from your Area/Cluster/Chapter to be the coach and captain of the team.
  6. ONLY PLAYERS LISTED ON THE OFFICIAL LINEUP WILL BE ALLOWED TO PLAY.
  7. Each team is required to wear shirts or jerseys of the same color corresponding to the assigned color of its delegation.
  8. Each team player is also required to have his or her jersey number visible on them at all times
  9. Each game will have two 10-minute halves (running time). There will be NO CLOCK STOPPAGE, except during timeouts (30 seconds each).
  10. There will be a 3-minute break after the first half.
  11. Each team is only allotted 1 time-out.

Violations

  • Improper conduct and foul language will not be tolerated.
  • All referees' decisions are final and are not disputable.
  • Hard fouls are prohibited, any player causing danger to other players may be removed at the referee's discretion.

Scoring and Judging

Basketball is scored based on game points. The team with the highest score at the end of regulation time wins. In case of a tie:

  • Overtime: 3-minute sudden death overtime period
  • If still tied, continue with additional 2-minute overtime periods until a winner is determined
  • Each team receives 1 additional timeout per overtime period

Sportsmanship Evaluation

While not scored, teams are evaluated on:

  • Respect for officials and opponents
  • Adherence to rules and spirit of the game
  • Positive encouragement and team support
  • Conduct of coaches and bench players

Teams displaying exceptional sportsmanship may be recognized with a Spirit Award (separate from competition results).

  6. Each team is required to wear shirts or jerseys of the same color corresponding to the assigned color of its delegation.
  7. Team must have 6 players on the court at all times. IMPORTANT: No more than 3 boys are allowed on the court at any given time. This means teams must have at least 3 girls playing on the court at all times.
  8. GAMEPLAY is as follows:
    1. Each game will consist of one set played up to 25 points.
    2. Teams win by at least 2 points.
    3. Finals:
      1. Best of 3 sets
      2. Sets go up to a 25-rally point system.
      3. If there is a tie (i.e., the teams win one set each), a deciding set will be played up to 9 points.
  9. Any form of protest should be coursed through the designated TEAM CAPTAIN. The Team Captain must then relay the concern to the Competitions' Administration Booth, where the situation will be dealt with in the fairest way possible.

Rules

  • Can volley off the serve
  • Foot under the net is considered a lost possession.
  • If the serve touches the net, the ball is still in play.

Time Limit and Gameplay

  • Each set has a maximum time limit of 15 minutes OR 25 points, whichever comes first
  • When less than 1 minute remains, the lead official will notify both team captains
  • At the 1-minute warning, the official will announce whether the set will:
    • Continue playing to 25 points (if achievable within time)
    • Reduce the target score (if 25 points is not achievable)
    • End at time expiration with the higher score winning
  • If time expires and scores are tied, play continues until one team wins by 2 points
  • Finals: Best of 3 sets, each with 15-minute time limit OR 25 points
  • Deciding set (if needed): 10 minutes OR 9 points, whichever comes first

Scoring and Judging

Volleyball is scored based on rally point system. The team that wins the required number of sets wins the match.

Sportsmanship Evaluation

While not scored, teams are evaluated on:

  • Respect for officials and opponents
  • Adherence to rules and spirit of the game
  • Positive encouragement and team support
  • Conduct of coaches and bench players

Teams displaying exceptional sportsmanship may be recognized with a Spirit Award (separate from competition results).
