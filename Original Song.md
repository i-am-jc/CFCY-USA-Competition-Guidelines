**ORIGINAL SONG COMPETITION** 

1\. All participants should submit their final entry form at least 2 weeks before the start of YCON*.* The Creatives Committee should be notified ahead of time for any changes that will be  made prior to the YCON date. **Any revisions made on the day of will not be accepted.** 

2\. There is no minimum or maximum to the number of members. 

3\. The official list should be verified by the **CFC-Youth Area/Cluster/Chapter Couple Coordinator  and/or Area/Cluster/Chapter Heads** to ensure that the members are **active CFC Youth  members**. An entry form missing an/a Area/Cluster/Chapter Couple Coordinator?s and/or  Area/Cluster/Chapter Head?s approval will not be accepted. 

4\. All participants must be registered for YCON. 

5. Written music must be of a positive and/or Catholic nature. The written music must be categorized in one of the following categories: *Praise (Fast Song)* or *Worship (Slow Song).* The lyrics must be written as if they are to be used as a potential song for CFC Youth.

6. The composition can be no longer than 5 minutes in length. 

7. Your song must somehow incorporate the CFC 2025 theme anchor verse of **Bible verse Matthew 28:8 & 10 NABRE**, which says, 

*?**8** Then they went away quickly from the tomb, fearful yet overjoyed, and ran to announce[[a]](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%2028%3A8-10&version=NABRE#fen-NABRE-28523a) this to his disciples? **10** Then Jesus said to them, ?Do not be afraid. Go tell my brothers to go to Galilee, and there they will see me.?*

8. No additional dancers nor additional non-band members are allowed during the showcase. 

9. Upon sign-up, you will indicate exactly what instruments you will be using, as well as how many people will be needed to orchestrate your musical piece.

10. All writers must submit a minimum **1-paragraph write-up** about the inspiration that they had creating this piece and the process. This should be submitted along with the *mp3 recording* of the song and a presentation with lyrics at least 2 weeks before the start of YCON via email to [cfcycomps@gmail.com](mailto:cfcycomps@gmail.com) for the Creatives Committee. 

11. Also, please provide a printed copy of the lyrics for the Creatives Committee the day of YCON with the **song name, writer(s), and the order of the verses**. 

\*\*\* The mp3 recording should be as clean as possible. The presentation slides should have a plain white background with the text in black. 

12. The lyrics of the song must be handed to the Creatives Committee at least one hour before the showcase for them to have a chance to read the lyrics beforehand. 

13. The song that is performed must be the same as the lyrics handed to the Creatives Committee. 

14. It is the responsibility of the performer(s) to signify his/her/their presence at least **one hour** before the showcase. He/She/They need(s) to present a copy of the music lyrics to the Creatives Committee at least one hour before the performance. Participants must pay attention to any announcements during YCON. Their performance order will be announced before the showcase. 

**Criteria for Judging** 

**Performance-based Criteria** 

**Music Rendition (Musical Creativity) 10%** 

**Musical Arrangement 30%** 

Effective use of instruments 10% 

Transitions (Climax, Pause, Flow, etc.) 10% 

Stage Presence 10% 

**Showmanship 20%** 

On-stage presence 10% 

Confidence 10% 

**Message and Content 40%** 

Uniqueness of Lyrics 20% 

Content 20% 

***TOTAL 100%*** 

**DEDUCTIONS** 

? Late entry form submission, including 1-paragraph write-up (5 points) 

? Over the time limit (2 points) 

? Failure to provide lyrics (presentation) and mp3 (5 points) 

? Performance does not reflect lyrics given (5 points) 

? Not of Christian nature (5 points) 

? Deductions reasons and points will be marked in the judges? forms before the forms are handed  to them. 

**Note: Judges are required to give an overall score and then deduct points if necessary.**
