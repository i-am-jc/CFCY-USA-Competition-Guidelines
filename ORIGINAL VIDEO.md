Create an original video that can be used as a way to evangelize ? by showing them on social media  platforms and at varying CFCY events. 

**RULES & REGULATIONS** 

1. The original video must somehow interpret the CFC 2025 theme anchor verse of **Bible verse Matthew 28:8 & 10 NABRE**, which says, 

*?**8** Then they went away quickly from the tomb, fearful yet overjoyed, and ran to announce[[a]](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%2028%3A8-10&version=NABRE#fen-NABRE-28523a) this to his disciples? **10** Then Jesus said to them, ?Do not be afraid. Go tell my brothers to go to Galilee, and there they will see me.?*

2\. **Accepted original video submissions types:** 

a. Narrative \- Tells a fictional or fictionalized story, event or narrative. E.g. Spirit Rangers b. Awareness video \- The goals of an awareness video include: Draw attention to key issues that  matter to you, raise awareness about who you are and what you do, inspire people to act,  educate people, share a story of change, share first hand accounts.  

c. Commercial \- A video that usually promotes a product, service or brand. E.g. Cingular Parody,  Becoming a Catholic Nun 

3\. The Original Video can be as short as 1 minute, but it must be under 3 minutes and 15 seconds. 

5\. No one over 21 years of age is allowed to help with any filming, editing, or production. 

6\. There is no age limit for the persons *in* the film (e.g., grandparents, parents, etc.).

7\. REMINDER: Before filming *inside* a Church, ask permission from the parish or priest. Always be  respectful and reverent inside a Church ? Jesus is truly present in the Blessed Sacrament.
