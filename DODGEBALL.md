**DODGEBALL**

**Dynamics** 

1\. Gather all participants that wish to play and separate into teams of 10\. 

2\. First two teams will play one game until the first team loses all team members.  3\. First team to win stays on the court and the next team comes on. 

4\. All participants must be registered for the YCON. 

5\. This competition is open to active CFC Youth members within the age range of 12 to 18 years old (as of the date of the YCON).

6\. Games are subject to Sports Competitions Head; ex. If there are multiple courts available games  can be split in two. 

7\. Games can be co-ed or boys/girls. 

8\. **The winners of this will not ?advance? to compete in national**. It is a game to add if time  permits in the schedule. If dodgeball is played at YCON, it will also be as an optional  participatory game.
