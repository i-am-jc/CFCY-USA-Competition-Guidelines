**BAND COMPETITION**

1\. All participants should submit their final entry form at least 2 weeks before the start of YCON. The Creatives Committee should be notified ahead of time for any changes that will be  made prior to the YCON date. **Any revisions made on the day of will not be accepted.** 

2\. The **CFC-Youth Area/Cluster/Chapter Couple Coordinator and/or Area/Cluster/ Chapter Heads** must verify the official list to ensure they are **active CFC Youth members.** An entry form missing  an/a Area/Cluster/Chapter Couple Coordinator?s and/or Area/Cluster/Chapter Head?s approval  will not be accepted. This must be sent to the Creatives Committee via email. 

3\. All participants must be registered for YCON.  

4\. The band must consist of instrumentalists and vocalists. There is no minimum or maximum to  the amount of instrumentalists and vocalists, but there is a limit to the amount of inputs and  microphones. Amps will not be switched out, so if the team has an instrument requiring a  different plug-in, a vocal microphone must be used instead. 

5\. **Members of the band should be within the age bracket of 12 to 21 years old (as of the date of  the YCON).** 

6\. The Creatives Committee will provide as part of the sound system all amplifiers and **at least 1 bass, 2 acoustic guitars, 1 electric guitar, 1 keyboard, 6 vocal microphones, and 1 set of drums  per participating band.** The participating bands must provide for additional instruments and  other gadgets if necessary (e.g., violin, viola, trumpet, flute, saxophone, ukulele). 

\*If your band instrumentalist prefers to use his or her own instrument or has specific instrument/equipment use, please notify the Creatives Committee far in advance before the competition. Instruments that require a different amp than the ones provided must use a vocal microphone instead.

7\. Each band is required to play a medley of 3 songs. The order in which these will appear is up to the  discernment of the band. 

a. Remixed Worship Song (not performed in its original composition) 

8. Secular Songs: 
   i. One of the secular songs must be about the CFC 2026 theme "As One" anchor verse of **1 John 1:3-4**, which says, 

   **3** What we have seen and heard we announce to you also, so that you will join with us in the fellowship that we have with the Father and with his Son Jesus Christ. **4** We write this in order that our joy may be complete.

   ii. One of the secular songs must be reinvented in its format. (i.e., change of beat, fast to slow song, added instrumentals, etc.) 

   *Lyrics absolutely cannot have vulgarity or insinuations of anything ungodly.* 

9\. Presentation should be a minimum of 5 to a maximum of 6 minutes.  

10\. Each band will also be given 2 minutes to set up before they perform. This could take place during another performance. 

11\. The band look, which includes the attire of the members, must be decent and proper. 

12\. Music lyrics should be provided for all songs selected. **A copy of each song** will be needed for the Creatives Committee on the day of the competition. Only the lyrics being used are required. 

13\. It is the band?s responsibility to signify its presence at least **one hour** before the competition. The  band needs to present its music lyrics to the Creatives Committee at least one hour before its  performance. Participants must pay attention for any announcements during YCON. Their performance order will be announced before the competition. 

14\. All team captains must submit a minimum **1-paragraph write-up** explaining how the song  choices exemplify the **anchor verse**. This will be sent via email to the Creatives Committee at  least 2 weeks before the start of YCON.
