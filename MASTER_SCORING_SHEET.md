# MASTER SCORING SHEET GUIDE
## CFC Youth USA 2026 Competition Guidelines

This document explains how to calculate final weighted scores from judge scoresheets.

---

## HOW TO CALCULATE WEIGHTED SCORES

**Step 1:** Judges rate each criterion on a 1-10 scale
**Step 2:** Calculate the raw score for each criterion
**Step 3:** Apply the weight multiplier to get the final weighted score
**Step 4:** Sum all weighted scores for the total score out of 100

## TIPS FOR SCORE ADMINISTRATORS

1. **Use a spreadsheet** - Create an Excel/Google Sheets template with formulas pre-set for multipliers
2. **Average multiple judges** - If you have 3 judges, average their weighted scores for each criterion
3. **Apply deductions last** - Calculate the full weighted score first, then subtract deductions
4. **Round to nearest 0.5** - For consistency, round final scores to the nearest half point
5. **Document everything** - Keep all judge scoresheets and calculations for transparency

---

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: What if a judge gives a score of 0?**
A: The minimum score should be 1. If something is truly disqualifiable, use the deduction/disqualification system instead.

**Q: Can judges give decimal scores like 7.5?**
A: No, judges should only use whole numbers 1-10. Decimals come from the weighted calculation.

**Q: What if judges' scores are vastly different?**
A: If one judge's scores deviate significantly (>2 points difference on multiple criteria), discuss with judges to ensure they understand the rubric. You may need to discard outlier scores if there are 4+ judges.

**Q: How do we handle ties?**
A: First tiebreaker: Higher score in "Theme Relevance" category. Second tiebreaker: Higher raw scores (before weighting). Third tiebreaker: Judges' deliberation.
